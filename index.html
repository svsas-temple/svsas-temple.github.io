Sacred Shrines

The Holy Presiding Deities

🪔

Three divine energies blessing devotees with wisdom, courage, health, and spiritual liberation.

Vignaharta

Lord Sri Vinayaka

శ్రీ వినాయక స్వామి (విఘ్నేశ్వరుడు)

The remover of all hurdles and granter of intellect and auspicious beginnings. Devotees offer Modakas, Garika (durva grass), and coconut deepams for success in education, business, and new ventures.

"Vakratunda Mahakaya Suryakoti Samaprabha | Nirvighnam Kuru Me Deva Sarvakaryeshu Sarvada"
🌺 Special Pujas: Sankatahara Chaturthi, Vinayaka Chavithi Brahmotsavam
Velayudha

Lord Sri Subramanya Swamy

శ్రీ సుబ్రమణ్య స్వామి (కార్తికేయ / మురుగన్)

The divine commander of celestial forces, embodying radiant valor and healing energy. Devotees worship Lord Murugan with his holy Vel (spear) for courage, Kuja Dosha Nivarana, and family peace.

"Om Saravana Bhavaya Namaha | Shadananaya Vidmahe Subramanyaya Dheemahi"
🌺 Special Pujas: Skanda Sashti, Subrahmanya Sashti, Krittika Deepam
Dharma Sastha

Lord Sri Ayyappa Swamy

శ్రీ అయ్యప్ప స్వామి (హరిహరపుత్ర / ధర్మశాస్త్ర)

The embodiment of ascetic discipline, purity, and universal equality. Lord Ayyappa grants refuge to all devotees undertaking the sacred 41-day Mandala Deeksha, Irumudi Kattu, and Padi Pooja.

"Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa | Harivarasanam Viswamohanam"
🌺 Special Pujas: Mandala Makaravilakku Mahotsavam, Padi Pooja, Annadanam
